### The Silent Crisis in Feline Medicine
Cats are notorious masters of disguise. In the wild, showing vulnerability or sickness invites predators; as a consequence, domestic felines biologically suppress signs of pain and illness until pathological damage is severe and often irreversible.

My cat Michelin was the love of my life. He suffered from a health condition that wasn't detected in time. That profound heartbreak inspired a singular question: **Can we build an autonomous, collaborative AI agent that catches what humans might miss before it's too late?**

That is why we built **Michelin**, an intelligent, multimodal veterinary clinical co-pilot designed for the **Collaborative Partner Track** of the *All Things Agentic Hackathon*.

---

### Beyond Chatbots: The Power of Collaborative Agency
Traditional AI chatbots wait passively for questions. But in a fast-paced veterinary clinic where doctors juggle dozens of patients a day, passive search tools are insufficient.

Michelin introduces **Bidirectional Collaborative Inquiry**:
1. **Longitudinal Synthesis**: Instead of looking at isolated visits, Michelin fuses historic radiographs, blood chemistry trends (e.g. Albumin:Globulin ratio, SDMA, BUN), ultrasound findings, and owner behavioral logs into a continuous timeline.
2. **False-Negative Sentinel**: When a cat presents with vague lethargy, Michelin automatically evaluates high-risk feline conditions (such as Feline Infectious Peritonitis [FIP], Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy [HCM], and early Chronic Kidney Disease [CKD]).
3. **Proactive Questioning**: Michelin asks the veterinarian targeted, high-yield diagnostic questions—such as checking for fluid waves, conducting a Rivalta test, or assessing resting respiratory rate.

---

### The Technical Stack
Michelin was architected from the ground up for robustness and low latency:
* **Google Gemini 2.5/3.5 Pro Multimodal**: Ingests thoracic radiographs, CBC panels, and consultation notes simultaneously.
* **Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) & Antigravity Runtime**: Orchestrates tool calling, guideline retrieval, and iterative clinical reasoning.
* **Google Cloud Run**: Hosts our microservice backend with automatic scaling and zero idle overhead.
* **Google Cloud Firestore & Cloud Storage**: Securely stores longitudinal patient records and high-resolution DICOM imaging.

---

### Real-World Testing in Aguascalientes
We are proud to share that Michelin is actively being piloted with real clinical cases at a veterinary clinic in Aguascalientes, Mexico. Early feedback highlights that the False-Negative Sentinel reduces differential formulation time while surfacing critical confirmatory tests within seconds.

By combining Google Cloud's serverless infrastructure with Gemini's multimodal reasoning, we are turning retrospective grief into proactive, life-saving clinical partnerships.
